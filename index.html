Theresearcher

I want to know why a model decides what it decides. My first paper tests three gradient-boosting models for phishing detection on four datasets, then checks whether their SHAP explanations hold up under attack.

How a model ships
  1. TrainPyTorch, TensorFlow or scikit-learn, usually with transfer learning
  2. ExplainSHAP or LIME to check what the model actually relies on
  3. Shrinkexport to ONNX, then quantize, prune or distill
  4. ServeFastAPI or Flask in Docker, deployed on Render
